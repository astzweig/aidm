Say how AI helped build your software.

AI Development Mode gives repositories a simple, human-readable way to disclose how much agency humans and generative AI had during development — without fake percentages or line-by-line attribution.

Named modes, not arbitrary levels.

A mode describes the typical workflow for substantive changes. It is not a quality score and no mode is inherently better than another.

Autonomous

Goal → software

AI performs substantially the complete development process after the initial goal or specification.

Supervised

AI owns large steps

AI independently handles substantial multi-step work while humans review milestones and redirect.

Collaborative

Build together

Human and AI shape decisions and implementation together through frequent feedback loops.

Directed

Human sets the path

The human defines requirements, architecture, and approach; AI executes bounded tasks.

Assistive

AI supports

The human performs core development; AI helps with debugging, tests, docs, review, and suggestions.

Incidental / None

Local or no AI use

AI is only used occasionally for small local tasks, or is intentionally not used at all.

Mixed

Different workflows? Say so.

When materially different workflows are common, use Mixed with a profile or publish scoped declarations instead of averaging them into a meaningless number.

One badge. More detail when it matters.

The badge communicates quickly. An optional .aidm.json profile describes planning, architecture, implementation, testing, review frequency, and the largest scope AI can handle autonomously.

Not authorship tracking. AIDM describes the development process. It does not claim which individual lines came from a model.

Open by design.
