# Governance
AIDM uses lightweight, open governance suitable for an early-stage public specification.

## Goals
Maintainers should optimize for:

- clarity for ordinary developers,
- stability of published mode meanings,
- vendor neutrality,
- low adoption friction,
- and honest representation of diverse human–AI workflows.

## Decision making
Changes are discussed through issues and pull requests. Normative changes should remain open long enough for meaningful community feedback when practical.

Maintainers may merge editorial fixes directly. Changes that alter classification meaning should be documented in `CHANGELOG.md` and reflected in the specification version.

## Versioning
AIDM follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org). See the specification for the meaning of major/minor/patch changes.

## Compatibility principle
Once AIDM reaches 1.0, a mode name should not silently change meaning. A breaking reinterpretation requires a new major version.

## Stewardship
The project began as part of **Astzweig's Social Contribution efforts**. The intention is for the specification to remain open, reusable, and receptive to community participation rather than becoming vendor-specific marketing.
