# Code of Conduct
We want discussion around AI-assisted development to be useful even when participants have strongly different views about AI tools.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss workflows and evidence rather than attacking people,
- avoid framing AIDM modes as moral or skill rankings,
- welcome questions from people with different levels of experience,
- give constructive, specific feedback,
- and respect reasonable requests to stop disruptive or harassing behavior.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.
Repository maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.
